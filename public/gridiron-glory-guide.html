Overview
What is Gridiron Glory?
Gridiron Glory is a private college football fantasy league platform for friend groups. Unlike professional fantasy sports apps, it's built specifically around the CFB calendar — draft teams in the summer, score points every Saturday through bowl season, and crown a champion after the National Championship game.
🐍
Live Snake Draft
Everyone joins the draft room at the same time. Picks happen in real-time — when it's your turn, the room lights up and you pick from the available teams.
⭐
Weekly Captain Picks
Each week you designate one captain from your roster. Their points are doubled. Each team can only be captain twice per season — choose wisely.
📡
Live Game Data
Scores, rankings, and stats pull automatically from the College Football Data API every 30 minutes during the season. No manual entry needed for weekly results.
🏆
Full Postseason
Scoring runs through the National Championship. Bowl games, CFP rounds, conference championships, Heisman Trophy — every milestone counts.
📊
Roster Analytics
A built-in analytics dashboard shows roster quality, best/worst picks, over/under draft value, and weekly scoring trends with heat-map comparisons.
🔒
Private Leagues
Leagues are invite-only. The commissioner generates a personal link for each player — nobody can join without an invitation.
Getting Started
Setting Up Your League
One person creates the league and becomes the commissioner. That's it — no app store download, no subscription. Visit the web app, sign up with your email, and you're ready.
1
Sign Up
Visit the app URL, click Sign Up, enter your name and email. Verify your email — takes 30 seconds.
2
Create League
Name your league, choose number of players and teams per player. You become commissioner automatically.
3
Invite Players
Generate invite links in the Admin panel. Send each link to a player — they sign up and join automatically.
4
Draft Day
When everyone's ready, set the draft order and click Start Draft. All players pick in real time.

Player Onboarding
Inviting Players to Your League
Once your league is created, head to the Admin panel and generate invite links. Each link is unique and single-use — when a player clicks it and signs up, they're added to your league automatically.
1
Commissioner generates link
Admin → Members & Invites → enter email → click Invite. A unique link appears instantly.
2
Copy & share the link
Click Copy and send it via text, email, or group chat. No email server needed.
3
Player signs up
They click the link, create an account, verify email, and land directly in the league.
4
Ready to draft!
Once all players have joined, commissioner sets draft order and starts the draft.
💡
Multi-league support: Players can belong to multiple leagues. A league switcher in the header lets anyone toggle between leagues they've joined. You can run separate leagues for different friend groups with the same account.
The Draft
Live Snake Draft Room
Everyone opens the Draft Room at the same time. The commissioner sets the pick order and clicks Start Draft. From there, picks happen in real-time — every player sees every pick the moment it's made.
📋
Conference Draft Rules: Each player must draft at least 2 teams from each P4 conference (SEC, Big Ten, Big 12, ACC) and no more than 3 teams from any single P4 conference. There are no limits on G5, Independents, or Pac-12 teams. The app enforces these rules automatically during the draft.

Points System
How Scoring Works
Teams earn and lose points every week based on game results. Ranked wins stack — beating a Top 5 team earns all four win bonuses simultaneously. Opponent rankings are locked at the time the game was played and never change retroactively.
📌
Points stack on ranked wins. Example: Louisville beats #2 Ohio State → +1 (Win) +1 (Beat Ranked) +2 (Top 15) +3 (Top 5) = +7 points, or +14 with Captain.

End of Season
Postseason Bonuses
All postseason bonuses are awarded manually by the commissioner in the Admin panel under the Postseason Bonuses tab. Points apply to the leaderboard instantly when awarded. The commissioner should award these as each milestone occurs throughout bowl season and the CFP.
⚙️
How to award a bonus: Admin → Postseason Bonuses tab → select the player, bonus type, and team → click Award Bonus. The default point value pre-fills automatically but can be adjusted. Add an optional note (e.g. "SEC Championship win") for reference.

Season Calendar
How the Year Flows
Gridiron Glory runs the full CFB calendar — from preseason setup through the National Championship in January.
July–August
League Setup & Draft
Commissioner creates the league, invites players, and everyone joins. Schedule a draft day — all players log in simultaneously, commissioner sets the order, and the live snake draft runs. Each player drafts 10 teams (min 2 / max 3 per P4 conference).
Late August – November
Regular Season
Scores update automatically every 30 minutes on game days. Each week players set their Captain before games kick off. The leaderboard updates live throughout Saturday. Commissioner advances the week counter in the Admin panel each Monday.
Early December
Conference Championship Week
Conference title games are automatically scored. After all conference championship games are played, the statistical ranking bonuses (Top/Bottom 3 in QBR, TDs, INTs, Sacks) lock in permanently. Commissioner awards Conference Championship and bowl eligibility bonuses in the Admin panel.
December – January
Bowl Season & CFP
Commissioner awards bowl game results, Heisman bonuses, and CFP round milestones manually as they happen. The app tracks all 4 CFP rounds with separate point values for each round reached.
Mid January
National Championship & Final Standings
CFP Championship winner bonus (+25) is awarded. Any coach firing bonuses (−10) must be awarded before the NC game. Final leaderboard standings are official — champion is crowned.
February – July
Offseason
The app shows final 2025 season data. Commissioner can reset the draft in August to start a new season. Player accounts and league history carry over — no need to start from scratch.
Admin Tools
Commissioner Controls
The commissioner has exclusive access to the Admin panel. This is where you manage members, award postseason bonuses, adjust scoring, and control the draft.
👥
Members & Invites
Generate invite links for new players, view who's in the league, and advance the current week counter. Each invite is unique and single-use.
🎁
Postseason Bonuses
Award all postseason bonuses manually — bowl results, CFP rounds, Heisman, conference championships, coach firings, Fulmer Cup suspensions. Points apply instantly to the leaderboard.
⚙️
Scoring Settings
Adjust the point values for any scoring category — wins, ranked wins, G5 losses, and more. Changes apply to all weeks retroactively.
🔄
Reset Draft
If the draft needs to be redone, the commissioner can wipe all picks and reset to pre-draft status. Requires double confirmation. Captain picks and bonuses are not affected.
📋
Commissioner weekly checklist: Every Monday — (1) advance current week in Admin, (2) check for any G5 upsets to verify scoring, (3) during postseason, award any bowl results or CFP advancement bonuses from the weekend.
